# Welcome to Argus

Find a token, create your own, and understand fees and rewards.

## The basics

Argus is a token launchpad on Arc. The app brings token discovery, launch settings,
market activity and holder rewards into one place. Prices and trading volume are
shown in USDC.

You can explore tokens without connecting a wallet. Connect to create a token, trade
or claim rewards.

## Choose your starting point

- **Find a token** — Search by name, ticker or address, then take a closer look at
  the token page. → [`02-discover-tokens.md`](02-discover-tokens.md)
- **Create a token** — Prepare the identity, choose the settings, and review your
  token before creating it. → [`03-create-token.md`](03-create-token.md)

## Understand the details

Every token has its own settings. Check its address, buy and sell tax, allocation and
market activity before deciding what to do next.

- **After you launch** — Follow your launch, open your token page, and know what to
  do if it takes longer. → [`04-launch-status.md`](04-launch-status.md)
- **Fees & rewards** — Understand buy and sell tax, the allocation, and the holder
  rewards shown on a token page. → [`05-fees-and-rewards.md`](05-fees-and-rewards.md)
- **Glossary** — A quick reference for the labels you will see while browsing and
  launching tokens. → [`06-glossary.md`](06-glossary.md)

## Using this guide

Choose a topic or search with ⌘K on Mac and Ctrl K on Windows or Linux. Use "On this
page" to jump to a section.

> **Before you confirm.** Check the token's contract address and review the
> transaction in your wallet before approving it.
