# Find a token

Source: https://argus.world/docs/discover-tokens

Search by name, ticker or address, then take a closer look at the token page.

## Search the market

On the Tokens page, click the search bar or press ⌘K / Ctrl K. Enter a token name,
ticker or its full contract address. Tickers work with or without the `$` prefix.

- **Open a token** — Use the arrow keys to move through matches, then press Enter.
  Clicking a result also opens its token page.
- **See the full result set** — Choose "View all matches" to apply the search to the
  token list. The search panel shows up to eight matches at a time.
- **Close the search** — Press Escape to return to browsing.

> **The address is the identifier.** Names and tickers can look alike. Use a full
> contract address when you need to find a specific token, and check the address on
> its page.

## Narrow the results

| Search scope | What it includes |
|---|---|
| All tokens | Every matching token in the available list. |
| Live | Tokens with a live status. |
| Graduating | Live tokens at or above 50% of their milestone progress. |
| Graduated | Tokens marked as having reached their milestone. |

If a search comes back empty, switch to "All tokens" and check the spelling or full
address.

## Read the token page

1. **Check the identity** — Compare the token name, ticker, address and creator links
   with the token you intended to open.
2. **Look at activity** — Read the price chart, recent trades, 24-hour volume and
   holder count together. Each describes a different part of the market.
3. **Inspect the settings** — Review both tax rates and the allocation. A dividends
   allocation of zero means the token does not direct tax to holder dividends.
