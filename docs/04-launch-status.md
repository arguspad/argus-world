# After you launch

Follow your launch, open your token page, and know what to do if it takes longer.

## From review to token page

Confirm the launch in your wallet, then wait for your token page to become
available.

1. **Review** — Check your settings.
2. **Confirm** — Approve in your wallet.
3. **Wait** — Wait for your token page.
4. **Ready** — Open your token page.

Token page taking longer? Keep the dialog open and choose **Check again** to refresh
its status. Checking the page again does not create another token.

## When the page is ready

Your token page opens automatically once it is ready.

- **View token** — Open the page immediately.
- **Stay here** — Cancel the automatic navigation. You can still open the page when
  you are ready.

## If the page takes longer

Your token page may take a moment to appear after launch. Keep the dialog open to
check its progress. Choose "Check again" to refresh the page status — this does not
create another token.

## If creation cannot complete

If the launch cannot complete, read the message before choosing "Try again". Your
details are saved in the dialog.

- **Wallet or network changed** — Reconnect the intended creator wallet on the
  required network, then return to the review.
- **An unclear result** — If your wallet shows a submitted transaction, check its
  status before trying to create again. A missing page alone does not establish
  whether a transaction succeeded.

## If a direct link shows no token

"No token at this address" means the page could not find a token for that address.
Check the full link and address, then try again. If you are still in the launch
dialog, use its "Check again" action instead.

If you recently launched the token, check the transaction in your wallet. A missing
page does not mean the launch failed.
